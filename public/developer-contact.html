Easy Receipt

A practical toolkit for issuing receipts, managing products and stock, serving customers at the point of sale, and keeping clear credit account records.

See app footage Contact the developer

A CLEARER DAILY WORKFLOW

From product scan to customer record

Easy Receipt brings common shop tasks into one browser-based workspace. Create and share receipts, work with a product catalog, track stock changes, and review customer balances and credit activity.

  • Build, print, download, and share itemized receipts.
  • Use barcode entry and point-of-sale checkout.
  • Maintain stock counts and managed product prices.
  • Keep customer balances and credit activity together.
